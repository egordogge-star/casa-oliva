Our story

Rooted in Puglia.

The best meals begin with a few good ingredients and someone who knows what to do with them.

Casa Oliva takes its cue from the kitchens of southern Italy. We roll our pasta by hand, cook with the seasons and bring dishes to the table as they’re ready. There’s always bread for the last of the sauce.

Good to know.

Is Casa Oliva a real restaurant?

Casa Oliva is a fictional portfolio project inspired by Puglia. The menu, hours and photographs illustrate the restaurant concept.

What happens when I preview a reservation?

You’ll see a summary of the date, time and party size in your browser. Your choices stay on this page and no real table is booked.

Are there vegetarian dishes on the menu?

Yes. The focaccia barese and aubergine parmigiana are vegetarian options on our sample menu.
